*This page is a work in progress.*

TLUG technical meetings run on presentations, lightning talks, and demos from
members — people like you. You don't need to be an expert or a polished
speaker: our meetings are small and casual, and first-time presenters are
very welcome.

## What we're looking for

- **Presentations** (20–40 minutes) — a deeper dive into a topic: Linux,
  free/open source software, hardware, networking, or anything the community
  would find interesting.
- **Lightning talks** (~5 minutes) — a quick show-and-tell: a tool you like,
  a problem you solved, a project you're hacking on.
- **Demos** — show something running; no slides required.

Talks may be given in English or Japanese.

## How to submit

Watch the [meetings page](/meetings/) — when a Call for Presenters is open it
is announced there and on the [mailing list](/mailing-lists/). To propose a
talk, send a short description (title, rough length, language) to the
mailing list.
