## Sponsoring TLUG

TLUG has been bringing Tokyo's Linux and free software community together
since 1994. Our events draw an international, technical crowd — engineers,
sysadmins, and enthusiasts working in and around Tokyo — and are held in
English and Japanese.

Ways organizations support us:

- **Venue space** — our most common need: a room for roughly 20–40 people on
  a weekend afternoon, ideally with a projector or large display.
- **Food and drink** — for the konshinkai (social) portion of meetings.
- **Auction items** — hardware, books, swag, and other (preferably
  Linux/FOSS/tech related) items for the meeting auction that helps fund
  event supplies.

What sponsors receive:

- Your logo and link on this page and the TLUG homepage.
- A thank-you on the meeting page and at the event itself.
- The chance to briefly introduce your organization at meetings you host —
  and our genuine gratitude.

Interested? Get in touch on the [mailing list](/mailing-lists/) or any of
our [chat channels](/chat/).
