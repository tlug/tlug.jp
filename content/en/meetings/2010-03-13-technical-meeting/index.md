## Summary

An open meeting given over to discussing the group's direction rather than
a single presentation. Members discussed broadening the range of session
formats beyond full-length talks to include lightning talks and tutorials,
and floated giving different months over to different formats, on the view
that shorter slots would draw more people into presenting.
