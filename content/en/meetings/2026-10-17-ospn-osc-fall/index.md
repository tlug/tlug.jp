TLUG will have a booth at
[Open Source Conference 2026 Fall](https://event.ospn.jp/osc2026-fall/) in
Asakusa, handing out TLUG-themed items and offering Linux installation
assistance (an "Installfest"). The conference hosts many open source booths
and presentations. Drop by to enjoy the event, or help out at the booth.

The conference is on the 6th floor of the Taito Building and admission is
free. Please register on
[connpass](https://tlug.connpass.com/event/405486/) as either a **booth
volunteer** or **just visiting** so we know who to expect. The conference
also runs a mini stage of short talks, with a live stream planned.

## Installfest

Bring a machine you would like Linux installed on and we will help you get
it running. Please back up anything important beforehand.

The venue Wi-Fi is shared with every other booth and is not reliable, so
bring your installation media on a USB stick if you have a particular
distribution in mind. Do not count on downloading it at the venue.

## For booth volunteers

- Setup starts at 08:30, when the hall is unlocked and tables and chairs are
  arranged. Exhibitor reception and booth preparation run from 09:00 to
  10:00.
- Bring a power strip with your name on it. The venue provides enough power
  for one or two laptops per booth, and loaner strips are limited.
- Everything has to be hand-carried on the day. The venue cannot receive
  shipments in advance.
- Teardown runs from 16:00 to 17:00, and the hall must be empty by 17:00.

## After the conference

OSPN hosts an after-party from 17:30 to 19:30 at Uotami Asakusa Okuyama
Omairimachi, about eight minutes' walk from the venue. It costs ¥4,000
(¥2,000 for students with ID, or ¥5,000 if you would like to sponsor a
student), payable at the OSC reception desk by 15:00. Places are limited and
[advance registration](https://ospn.connpass.com/event/403624/) is required.
