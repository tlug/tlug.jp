All Linux lovers, and supporters of open source and free software, in the
Kanto area (or anywhere else) are invited to attend TLUG meetings.
**Membership is open to anyone**, and there are no membership dues or
entrance fees.

TLUG meetings are traditionally small and unstructured (very casual). Most of
the benefit of a meeting comes from chatting with other Linux users and
having informal discussions.

As a general rule, **Technical Meetings** are held on the 2nd Saturday of
odd-numbered months and **Nomikai** (social) meetings are usually held on the
2nd Friday of even-numbered months. There are, of course, exceptions, so it
is best to keep an eye on the [mailing list](/mailing-lists/) or check back
here as the date approaches.

Looking for a specific presentation? See the [talk archive](/talks/).
