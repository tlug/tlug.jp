This event is open to all Linux users, friends, and first-timers alike. Join
us to talk about all things Linux (and non-Linux) over a few cold ones. Even
if you're not a Linux user or LUG member, but want to hang out with Linux
people, please join!

## Special guests

- [Arthur Tyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Tyde), founder of the
  Bay Area Linux Users Group and Linuxcare.
- Matthew Rice, Executive Director of the Linux Professional Institute.

## Cost

Cost is split among attendees:

    General Cost = Total Cost / (Number of General Attendees + Number of Student Attendees / 2)
    Student Cost = General Cost / 2

A student ID is required for the student discount.
