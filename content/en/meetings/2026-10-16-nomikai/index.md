In support of [KDE's 30th Anniversary](https://kde.org/anniversaries/30/), the theme of this month's nomikai is [KDE](https://kde.org)!

This event is open to all Linux users, friends, and first-timers alike. Join us to talk about all things Linux (and non-Linux) over a few cold ones. Even if you're not a Linux user or LUG member, but want to hang out with Linux people, please join!

## Cost

Cost is split among attendees:

    General Cost = Total Cost / (Number of General Attendees + Number of Student Attendees / 2)
    Student Cost = General Cost / 2
