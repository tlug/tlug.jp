This event is open to all Linux users, friends, and first-timers alike. Join
us to talk about all things Linux (and non-Linux) over a few cold ones. Even
if you're not a Linux user or LUG member, but want to hang out with Linux
people, please join!

We're on the lower level: enter on the first floor and take the stairs down.

## Cost

Cost is divided evenly at the conclusion, so please be prepared to pay in
either cash or card (touch only). Meals at Zest Cantina Daikanyama average
somewhere around ¥3000.
