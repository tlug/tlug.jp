#### Date

July 7, 2016 (Thursday)

#### Time

19:00 - 21:00

#### Event

Eat and drink (primarily a social activity)

#### Location

Mori no Beer Garden @Jingu Gaien [1](http://mbg.rkfs.co.jp/)

address: 14-13 Kirigaoka-machi, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo (Jingu Gaien Outer Garden Playground)

map: <http://mbg.rkfs.co.jp/#location>

• 5 minutes walk from Shinanomachi Station (JR) • 5 minutes walk from Kokuritsukyogijo Station (Oedo line) • 10 minutes walk from Aoyama 1-chome station (Ginza line)

#### Cost

Men: 4,200 yen / Women: 3,900 yen (all you can eat BBQ/all you can drink for 2 hours)

### Details

This event is open to all Linux users and friends, and is to promote casual Linux (and non-Linux) related discussions over a few cold ones. Even if you're not a Linux user or a LUG member, but want to hang out with Linux people, please come.

To help us make sure that there will be room for everyone to seat, **please register** your attendance in [Doorkeeper](http://tlug.doorkeeper.jp/).

Even if you want to come at the last minute it's okay - we may have to squeeze a bit, but please come!
